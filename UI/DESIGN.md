## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, mission-critical operational intelligence environment tailored for Indian Railway operations managers, station controllers, freight logistics dispatchers, and travel concierges. It blends the structural rigors of enterprise logistics software with the fluid utility of consumer-grade journey planners.

### Aesthetic Foundation
The visual language merges **Corporate / Modern Enterprise Precision** with functional **Tactile Transit Glass**. It evokes absolute reliability, operational velocity, and high-trust AI forecasting. Deep midnight-slate backdrops ground the system, allowing vibrant locomotive blues to guide workflow execution, while warm saffron-amber accents signal operational friction, delay metrics, and congestion thresholds without visual panic.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Vigilant & Authoritative:** The interface conveys institutional stability and mathematical accuracy. Information is calibrated for zero-ambiguity split-second decisions.
- **Dynamic & Predictive:** Surfaces feature active telemetry pulses, route status breadcrumbs, and live delay confidence vectors rather than static status text.
- **National Scale & Modernity:** Draws respect from India's vast railway infrastructure while projecting futuristic algorithmic sophistication.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered for high-density legibility under varying terminal lighting conditions, balancing deep executive blues, signal-grade alerts, and crisp surface tiers.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#0D5C75` - Deep Indian Railways Teal/Blue):** Commands global application headers, primary metric triggers, active route tracks, primary navigation tabs, and system confirmations.
- **Secondary (`#E05A1B` - Saffron / Kinetic Amber):** The alert and predictive risk signal. Utilized for delay escalations (>30m delay), critical bottlenecks, AI confidence warnings, and search focus indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#1F8A70` - Signal Emerald):** Signifies on-time status, clear section signals, normal schedule adherence, and verified train clearance.
- **Neutral (`#121926` - Midnight Slate):** Serves as high-contrast typographic ink, side rail navigations, station nodes, and map control chrome.

### Surface System
- **App Canvas:** `#F4F6FA` delivers an airy, modern, anti-fatigue base for dense data grids and map layers.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card & Module Base):** `#FFFFFF` with razor-sharp structural divider borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Sub-panels & Flyouts):** `#F8FAFC` providing contrast against pure white cards.
- **Dark Inversion Tier (`#0B132B`):** Dedicated operational cockpit panels, map navigation left-rails, and night-shift telemetry monitors.

## Typography

The typography pairings unify **Inter** for systemic, multi-density prose and dashboard analytics with **JetBrains Mono** for specialized rail telemetry, train IDs, PNR numbers, platform allocations, and delta timings.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Inter (Primary UI & Analytics):** Deployed across headlines, body copy, tooltips, and general user interactions. Headings utilize tight negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to guarantee compact, punchy delivery of operational metrics.
- **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry & Codes):** Assigned exclusively to Train Numbers (`12951 NDLS-BCT RAJDHANI`), PNR records (`245-8910231`), Platform indices (`PF 04`), ETA/RTD countdowns, and latitude/longitude spatial coordinates. This prevents horizontal tabular jitter during real-time web-socket stream refreshes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an **Adaptive Fluid Dashboard Split-View** combining a persistent navigation sidebar, an interactive dynamic journey inspector drawer, and an expansive geospatial tracking viewport.

### Spatial Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Tri-pane layout comprising an 80px compressed icon rail (or 240px expanded navigation), a 420px fixed-width journey analytical inspection and search panel, and a fluid spatial route map canvas with floating floating telemetry overlays.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Bi-pane layout. Navigation collapses into an off-canvas drawer or bottom app bar. Inspection panel slides over the map with a toggleable 50/50 vertical or horizontal split.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-pane layout with bottom-sheet journey inspector cards docked over the full-bleed route track progression canvas, utilizing a snap-to-bottom gesture drawer.

### Rhythmic Grid
All margins and paddings adhere strictly to an 8-point harmonic base scale (with 4px micro-increments via `space-xs`). Dense operational data cards utilize `space-sm` (8px) interior gap tokens, while dashboard module separators utilize `space-lg` (24px) for distinct ocular parsing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines **crisp planar boundaries** with **tinted ambient shadows**, avoiding generic mud-grey drop shadows in favor of crisp transit-slate depths.

### Depth Stratification
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F4F6FA` completely flat background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Containers):** Surface `#FFFFFF` bounded by a 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`) with a faint ambient floor shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(18, 25, 38, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns):** Elevated interactive tiles with shadow `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(13, 92, 117, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(18, 25, 38, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Map Overlays & Inspection Cards):** Semi-opaque operational cards leveraging backdrop blur: background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`, shadow `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 25, 38, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Severe Crisis Interventions):** Deep elevation with shadow `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(18, 25, 38, 0.25)` paired with a soft scrim blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric precision through **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape language:
- Standard buttons, input controls, chips, and small cards: `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- Large analytics widgets, map overlay side-drawers, and modal windows: `16px` (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`).
- Journey progression pills, timeline badges, and metric status pips: `24px` (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem`) or fully circular where symmetrical.

This creates an intentional balance between industrial utilitarian firmness and modern, inviting touch targets.

## Components

### 1. Unified Search Input Bar (Train No / PNR / Station)
- **Structure:** Prominent multi-mode composite input field with integrated switch segment for `[Train No]`, `[PNR]`, and `[Station]`.
- **Styling:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, border-radius `8px`. Active focus shifts border to `#0D5C75` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 92, 117, 0.15)` glow ring.
- **Enhancements:** Leading icon representing the active mode (train icon, ticket icon, or station icon) with trailing inline keyboard shortcut badge `[Enter ↵]` in `JetBrains Mono`.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0D5C75`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight 600. Hover darkens to `#094254`. Active scale down `0.98`.
- **Critical / High Delay Action:** Background `#E05A1B`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover darkens to `#C44B13`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#121926`. Hover changes to background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Icon Utility Buttons:** 36x36px or 40x40px rounded squares for map zoom, layer toggles, and route filters.

### 3. Chips & Telemetry Status Badges
- **On-Time Badge:** Soft green fill `rgba(31, 138, 112, 0.1)`, text `#1F8A70`, dot indicator pulsating in green.
- **Moderate Delay (10–30m):** Saffron fill `rgba(224, 90, 27, 0.1)`, text `#E05A1B`.
- **Severe Delay (>30m):** Deep Crimson fill `rgba(217, 38, 38, 0.1)`, text `#D92626`.
- **Platform Pill:** Midnight slate container `#121926`, text `#FFFFFF` set in `JetBrains Mono` label-md.

### 4. Station Delay Timeline Graphs
- **Track Layout:** Vertical and horizontal stepper nodes linked via a 3px railway spline line.
- **Node Geometry:** Passed stations marked with solid primary blue nodes; delayed stations marked with secondary saffron halos; current live train position represented by a pulsing train locomotive marker.
- **Time Dials:** Scheduled Time crossed out in muted grey; Predicted Time highlighted prominently alongside delta tag (e.g., `+42m Late`).

### 5. AI Delay Confidence Gauge
- **Visual Pattern:** Compact semicircular or linear segmented meter (0-100%).
- **Color Mapping:** >85% (High Reliability) tinted `#1F8A70`; 60-84% tinted `#0D5C75`; <60% (Volatile Weather / Congestion) tinted `#E05A1B`.
- **Labeling:** Accompanying label stating model attribution (e.g., `92% Confidence • Historical & Monsoon Weighted`).

### 6. Cards & Metric Tiles
- **Architecture:** White base, subtle divider headers, embedded sparkline charts for historical 7-day puncture trends.
- **Header:** Title in Inter `headline-sm`, trailing telemetry timestamp in JetBrains Mono `body-sm`.
